Hello! This is a standard additions, aka spike, calculator that helps anyone who would like to calculate what a theoretical value is for adding a standard to a sample to test if there are interferences. This app will take your inputs and calculate what the theoretical
concentration will be and also calculate % recovery and whether it falls within the range of 85-115%. Be sure to choose a volume and concentration that requires a small amount of standard spike volume and a large enough concentration to make a 
significant difference between the original sample and the spiked concentration.
